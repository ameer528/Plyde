# Plyde

AI-powered client acquisition for freelancers and small agencies.

Plyde helps you find potential clients, research their businesses, and write personalized outreach. You review and approve every message before anything is sent.

## Status

Early development. Not yet launched.

## Planned features

- Business and ideal customer profile setup
- Prospect discovery and fit scoring
- Personalized email drafts
- Human approval before sending
- Sales pipeline with follow-up reminders

## Tech

Next.js, Supabase, and free-tier AI models.

## Principles

- You stay in control. Nothing is sent without your approval.
- Every fact about a prospect shows where it came from.
- No spam, no guessed emails.
